Take-Home Vehicle Program

Policy agreement for officers and deputies assigned a department take-home vehicle.

Officers & Deputies
Privilege — Not a Right
01

Purpose

The Take-Home Vehicle Program is intended to increase realism, accountability, vehicle ownership responsibility, and officer immersion while maintaining department standards and operational readiness.

02

Eligibility

  • Participation is a privilege, not a right.
  • Officers must be in good standing.
  • Officers must not have recent disciplinary action.
  • Command staff may approve or deny participation at their discretion.
  • Participation may be revoked at any time.
03

Vehicle Ownership

  • Vehicles remain the property of the department and government.
  • Officers are assigned a vehicle; they do not own it outright.
  • Vehicle assignments may be changed based on department needs.
  • Officers may be required to surrender their assigned vehicle upon request.
04

Officer Responsibilities

Officers assigned a vehicle shall:

  • Maintain the vehicle in good condition.
  • Keep the vehicle clean and presentable.
  • Ensure required equipment remains stocked and operational.
  • Report damage, theft, vandalism, or mechanical issues promptly.
  • Secure the vehicle when unattended.
  • Operate the vehicle professionally and within department policy.
05

Vehicle Modifications

  • All modifications require department approval.
  • Only approved liveries, equipment, lighting, and accessories may be installed.
  • Unauthorized modifications are prohibited.
  • Specialty vehicles require additional approval.
06

Vehicle Use

✓ May be used
  • While on duty.
  • While traveling to and from approved duty assignments.
  • During approved roleplay activities.
✕ May not be used
  • For personal criminal activity.
  • For unauthorized racing, stunting, or reckless operation.
  • For non-department purposes outside approved roleplay.
07

Damage & Reporting

Officers must immediately report:

  • Theft
  • Vandalism
  • Significant collisions
  • Missing equipment
  • Vehicle malfunctions
Failure to report incidents may result in disciplinary action.
08

Revocation of Privileges

Take-home privileges may be revoked for:

  • Failure to maintain the vehicle.
  • Repeated damage caused by negligence.
  • Misuse of department assets.
  • Policy violations.
  • Department disciplinary action.
  • Inactivity.
Required Before Purchase

Sign Before You Buy

Officers and deputies opt into the program by acknowledging this policy in their MDT. It must be signed before you can purchase a take-home vehicle.

1
Open your MDT in game.
2
Go to the Fleet tab and select Policy & Sign-Off.
3
Review the policy and acknowledge it.
4
Purchase your vehicle at the dealership right next to this display.
